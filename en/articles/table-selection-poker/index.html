Table Selection — Who and When to Play

Table selection is the cheapest strategic edge in poker — playing average on a good table beats playing well on a tough table. Baatryn Öröö runs online poker 24/7 with multiple open tables, so the choice is always yours.

Quick answer

Criterion Good table Bad table
VPIP High (plays many hands) Low (tight ranges)
Player type 2–4 weak players 5+ regulars
Pot size Large, multiway Small, heads-up
Time Evening, weekends Early morning

Picking a table = finding profitable opponents.

Why it matters

  1. ROI increases — weaker players = more profit
  2. Variance drops — tough tables create more swings
  3. Easier to learn — works even at micro stakes
  4. Saves time — leave a bad table and find a better one immediately

Cash game Mongolia — table selection matters more in cash games than tournaments.

5 steps to selecting a table

1. Browse the lobby

PPPoker app — review all open tables. Check blinds, player count, and average pot size.

2. Check VPIP

Statistics — a table-average VPIP of 25%+ is good (players see more flops). Below 15% means tight, difficult opponents.

3. Player count

  • 6-max Omaha — 4–5 players is ideal
  • 9-max Hold'em — 6–8 players, not overly full

4. Time of day

Mongolia evenings 20:00–02:00 — more activity, more recreational players. VIP tables — weekends especially.

5. Observe for 30 minutes

Sit down and watch 20–30 hands. If the table is too tight, leave and find another.

Signs of a good table

  • Frequent limps and calls
  • Big pots, multiway action
  • Players chatting — often recreational players
  • Blind steals succeeding

Reasons to leave a bad table

  • 5+ regulars (experienced, tight players)
  • All pots heads-up, little action
  • Your exploits are not working
  • Tilt — you have lost 2 buy-ins and are playing poorly, not because the table is bad (poker psychology)

By stake level

Limit Table selection
250/500 Many tables — more options
1K/2K – 5K/10K VPIP and pot size matter
10K/20K High stakes — more regulars, fewer options

Bankroll connection

A good table reduces variance. Bankroll management — avoid putting too many buy-ins into a tough table.

Mobile vs desktop

Mobile vs desktop — the lobby is easier to browse on desktop. Mobile is fine once you are seated.

FAQ

What if tables are empty?

Wait or try a different limit. Tables open around the clock at Baatryn Öröö.

How long to stay at one table?

2–4 hours, then reassess or take a break.

Is table selection rude?

No. It is the most commonly used advantage among professional players.
