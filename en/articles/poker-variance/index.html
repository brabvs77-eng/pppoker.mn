Variance — Long-Term Perspective

Variance is not a poker malfunction — it is short-term swing. You can play well and lose; you can play poorly and win. At Baatryn Öröö online poker, not understanding variance leads directly to tilt and bankroll destruction.

Quick answer

Variance
What is it? Short-term fluctuation in results
Long term Good players become profitable
Short term Any outcome is possible
Solution Bankroll, discipline

Variance is part of poker. It is not just bad luck.

Why does variance exist?

Poker is skill + luck. One hand, one session — luck dominates. Over 10,000 hands and a year — skill takes over.

Example: AA vs 72 — AA wins 80% of the time. Losing 20% = variance. Over 100 trials, AA wins most of the time.

Short-term vs long-term

Timeframe Variance What you see
1 session High -5 buy-ins or +10 buy-ins
1 month Moderate Swings, but a trend emerges
6+ months Low Profitable players show profit

Bankroll management — your bankroll must be deep enough to survive variance.

Variance by stake level

Limit Variance (one session)
250/500 2–5 buy-in swing
1K/2K 3–7 buy-in swing
10K/20K 5–15 buy-in swing

Higher limits = bigger swings. Be prepared.

Living with variance

1. Bankroll

Keep 30–50 buy-ins total. Max 2–3 buy-ins per table. Losing still leaves you in the game.

2. Mental game

Poker psychology — stop after 3 buy-ins lost. It is not bad luck, it is variance.

3. Statistics

Statistics — meaningful trends only appear after 1,000+ hands. 50 hands means nothing.

4. Table selection

Table selection — weaker tables = lower variance (and more profit).

5. Strategy

Pot odds, bet sizing — correct decisions overcome variance over time.

Bad luck vs mistake

Bad luck Mistake
AA lost to 72 Called with 72
Draw did not complete Played a weak hand poorly
One-time event Repeats regularly

Fix mistakes. Accept variance.

Downswing — what to do

  1. Stop — 3+ buy-ins lost, end the session
  2. Change table — table selection
  3. Drop down — move from high stakes to micro temporarily
  4. Review stats — check if VPIP, PFR have drifted
  5. Support — @BatrynOrooSupport

Upswing — stay alert

When running hot:

  • Do not move up too quickly
  • Do not change your strategy (becoming looser or more aggressive)
  • Protect your bankroll — the upswing will end

FAQ

How many buy-ins can variance cost?

Depends on the limit and play style. A 20–30 buy-in downswing is normal.

Can variance be reduced?

Table selection, tighter ranges, fewer multiway pots help. But variance cannot be eliminated entirely.

Tournament vs cash game variance?

Tournaments have higher variance. Cash game is more stable.
