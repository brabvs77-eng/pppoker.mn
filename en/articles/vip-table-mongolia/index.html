VIP Tables — Who Should Play and When

VIP tables mean higher blinds, stronger opponents, and bigger swings. At Baatryn Öröö, blinds go up to 10K/20K, with Omaha 6-max VIP tables running alongside high-stakes Hold'em. Ulaanbaatar evenings and weekends see the most action.

VIP is not status. It is a stake level. Move up only when your bankroll and skills justify it.

Stakes overview

Type Blinds Table
Micro 250/500 Hold'em 9-max
Low 500/1K – 2K/4K Hold'em, Omaha
Mid 5K/10K Hold'em
VIP 10K/20K Hold'em, Omaha 6-max

Cash games Mongolia — real-money chips, not tournaments.

Who belongs at VIP?

  1. Bankroll ready — 10K/20K needs 300K+₮ minimum (30 buy-ins at 100BB)
  2. Solid preflop and postflop fundamentals
  3. Profitable over a large sample at mid stakes (5K/10K)
  4. Emotional control — responsible gaming limits in place
  5. Understanding of bet sizing at deep stacks

Beginners start at 250/500. Do not «try» VIP to learn. You will lose faster than you learn.

Omaha VIP

Omaha 6-max at VIP = maximum action. Bigger pots, more draws, higher variance.

Requirements: Omaha rules + Hold'em vs Omaha comparison.

Bankroll: 50–70 buy-ins recommended for PLO. A bad session at 10K/20K can cost 200K+₮.

Peak hours

  • Mongolia evenings 20:00–02:00 — most regulars online
  • Weekends — higher volume
  • 24/7 tables exist — action varies by hour
  • Ask @BatrynOrooSupport for current VIP table names

Prepare before moving up

  1. Track VPIP and PFR — poker statistics
  2. Master table position at mid stakes
  3. Review pot odds on large river calls
  4. Win consistently at 5K/10K over 5,000+ hands
  5. Deposit enough for 30+ buy-ins — deposit guide

Payments and withdrawals

VIP chips = real money. Same cashier for deposits, USDT, and withdrawals.

Winning sessions mean larger withdrawals. Plan IBAN timing if you need funds same day.

What to expect

  • Tighter preflop ranges from regulars
  • More 3-bet pots
  • Less bluffing from unknowns (they have real hands more often)
  • Faster decisions expected — know poker terms

FAQ

Separate VIP bonus?

100% deposit bonus applies to all players — not VIP-exclusive.

Can you lose at VIP?

Yes. Higher limits = bigger swings. Even winning players have losing weeks.

Who will I play against?

Mongolian regulars, expats, and players from the wider PPPoker network.

When to move up from mid stakes?

30+ buy-ins for next level, profitable over large sample, and emotional readiness for bigger pots.

Downswing reality

At 10K/20K, losing 5 buy-ins in a week is 10 million ₮. Strong players experience this. Weak bankroll management turns a downswing into a disaster.

Stay at stakes where that number would not end your poker career. Responsible gaming and bankroll management matter more at VIP than anywhere else.
