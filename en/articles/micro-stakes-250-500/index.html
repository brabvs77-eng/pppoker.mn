Micro Stakes 250/500 — Surviving and Learning

250/500 is the lowest blind level at Baatryn Öröö — the starting point for learning online poker in Mongolia and building a bankroll. You can sit down for 10,000₮. But even micro stakes demand discipline.

Quick answer

250/500 micro
1 BB 500₮
20 BB buy-in 10,000₮
Purpose Learning, fixing leaks
Risk Low — but tilt is dangerous

Micro stakes = not big money, it is a strategy practice table.

Why start at 250/500?

  1. Low risk — losing one hand does not wipe out your bankroll
  2. Many tables — 24/7 Hold'em 9-max always running
  3. Cheap mistakes — practice preflop and postflop without heavy cost
  4. Build a roll — move up once you become profitable

Cash game Mongolia format — not a tournament, leave whenever you want.

Bankroll — how much to start with?

Bankroll BB at 250/500 Notes
10,000₮ 20 BB Bare minimum — one table
20,000₮ 40 BB Recommended — 2 buy-ins
50,000₮ 100 BB Comfortable play

Bankroll management: 20–30 buy-ins total is sufficient for micro stakes. Start with 10,000₮ and add more as profits grow.

5 rules for surviving micro stakes

  1. Do not play weak hands — follow starting hand selection strictly
  2. Position — playing in position wins more hands (table position)
  3. Tilt — leave the table if you lose 2–3 buy-ins (poker psychology)
  4. Table selection — avoid overly aggressive tables (table selection)
  5. Stats — track VPIP and winnings (statistics)

Common mistakes

  • Playing too many hands — even at micro you need to fold often
  • Defending the blinds — calling with weak holdings
  • Reloading repeatedly — buying in again and again after losses
  • Moving up too soon — jumping to 500/1K before you are ready

When to move up?

Conditions for moving to 500/1K or 1K/2K:

  • 3+ months at 250/500
  • 30+ buy-in bankroll
  • Profitable by statistics (or minimal loss)
  • Understanding variance

Moving up too quickly = losing your bankroll.

Hold'em or Omaha?

At micro stakes, Hold'em 9-max is better suited for beginners. Omaha 6-max has more action and bigger pots. Read Hold'em vs Omaha before switching.

FAQ

Can you make money at 250/500?

Yes, but the primary goal is to learn. Profitable players at this level move up over time.

How many hours should I play?

1–2 hours per day, free of tilt. Micro stakes is a marathon, not a sprint.

When can I play VIP?

VIP tables run at 10K/20K and are for experienced players. Master micro stakes first.
