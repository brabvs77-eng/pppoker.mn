Who belongs at high stakes?

  1. Bankroll — 30–50 buy-ins dedicated to high stakes (bankroll management)
  2. Strategy — solid preflop, 3-bet/4-bet, postflop
  3. Experience — 6+ months profitable at mid stakes
  4. Mental game — tolerance for big swings (psychology)

Beginners should not enter 10K/20K directly. Doing so goes against responsible gaming.

High stakes strategy

Preflop

  • Tighter ranges — reading ranges
  • 3-bets and 4-bets are more common
  • Position is critical — table position

Postflop

  • C-bet — more selective
  • Check-raise — more dangerous
  • Bet sizing — pot control matters more

Exploitation

  • Read opponents individually — adjust to tendencies
  • Poker statistics — use VPIP/PFR to inform decisions

Omaha high stakes

Omaha 6-max VIP means maximum action and larger pots. Study Omaha rules and Hold'em vs Omaha. Bankroll recommendation: 50–70 buy-ins.

Variance — be prepared

At high stakes, swinging 5–10 buy-ins in a single session is normal. Without understanding variance, tilt and bankroll destruction follow.

Table selection

Table selection matters more at high stakes — finding tables with weaker players. Peak activity: Mongolia evenings 20:00–02:00.
