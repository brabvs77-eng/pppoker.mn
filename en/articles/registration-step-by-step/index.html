Registration — Step by Step

Registering at Baatryn Öröö takes 5–10 minutes. Whether you are in Ulaanbaatar, Darkhan, Erdenet, or anywhere in Mongolia, follow this guide and you can sit at a real cash table today.

The mongol poker system runs on PPPoker. You need the app, an account, and club membership before you play.

What you need

  • Smartphone (Android / iOS) or computer
  • Stable internet connection
  • 10,000₮+ for your first deposit
  • Telegram for support — @BatrynOrooSupport

No credit card required for Mongolian players. IBAN bank transfer is the standard method.

Step 1: Download PPPoker

  1. Open the mongol poker download guide
  2. Android: APK from official source or Play Store
  3. iOS: App Store
  4. Windows / macOS: official PPPoker desktop client

Step 2: Create an account

  1. Open PPPoker
  2. Tap «Register» / Sign up
  3. Enter phone number or email
  4. Create a strong, unique password
  5. Enter SMS or email verification code

Use a password you do not reuse on other sites. See account security.

Step 3: Join the club

  1. Tap «Search club» or enter club ID manually
  2. Get the current Baatryn Öröö club ID from @BatrynOrooSupport
  3. Send a join request
  4. Approval usually takes a few minutes
  5. After approval, club tables appear in the lobby

Search terms: Baatryn Öröö, Баатрын Өрөө, or baatryn oroo. Confirm via official channels only.

Step 4: First deposit

  1. Open Cashier inside PPPoker
  2. Select IBAN transfer
  3. Follow on-screen instructions
  4. Transfer 10,000₮ or more from your Mongolian bank
  5. Funds arrive in 10 minutes to 4 hours

New players may qualify for the 100% deposit bonus. Read terms before depositing.

Step 5: Pick a game
