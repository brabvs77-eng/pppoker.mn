USDT (TRC20) Deposit — Complete Guide

USDT (TRC20) lets Mongolian players top up their online poker account with cryptocurrency. At Baatryn Öröö, USDT unlocks after 100,000₮+ total IBAN deposits.

IBAN works from day one. USDT is an optional faster path for players who already use crypto.

When does USDT unlock?

  1. Deposit via IBAN first (deposit guide)
  2. Total IBAN deposits reach 100,000₮ (cumulative, not one transfer)
  3. USDT (TRC20) appears in Cashier
  4. Future top-ups can use USDT or IBAN

This threshold verifies your account before crypto payments open.

Why TRC20?

  • TRC20 = Tron network — low transaction fees (often under $1)
  • Faster and cheaper than ERC20 (Ethereum)
  • Widely used among Mongolian crypto traders

Critical: Use TRC20 only. Sending on ERC20, BEP20, or other networks can mean permanently lost funds. The club cannot recover wrong-network transfers.

Step by step

  1. Open PPPoker → Cashier → USDT (TRC20)
  2. Club USDT address and QR code appear on screen
  3. Open your wallet (Trust Wallet, Binance, etc.)
  4. Send to that exact address — copy-paste, never type manually
  5. Enter memo/tag if the club instructions require it
  6. Wait 10–60 minutes — depends on Tron network congestion
  7. Balance updates in PPPoker cashier

Security

  • Copy-paste addresses only
  • Test with a small amount on first transfer
  • Follow only @BatrynOrooSupport instructions
  • Ignore fake Telegram bots promising «instant bonus»
  • Read account security

Never send USDT to an address from a random chat message.

IBAN vs USDT

IBAN (₮) USDT (TRC20)
Min. 10,000₮ Per club guide
Speed 10 min – 4 hours 10–60 min
Requirement None 100,000₮+ IBAN history
Withdrawal IBAN account Per club policy
Fees Bank may charge Network fee (low on TRC20)

Troubleshooting

  • No credit after 2 hours → send TxID (transaction hash) to support
  • Wrong network → often unrecoverable — double-check before sending
  • Amount below minimum → top up difference or ask support
  • Wrong memo → contact support immediately with TxID

After USDT deposit

Play as normal at any stake. 100% deposit bonus terms may apply to qualifying deposits.

Withdrawals: see withdrawal issues for timing and common problems.

FAQ

Must 100,000₮ be one transfer?

No. It is the total of all IBAN deposits. Split across multiple transfers is fine.

Can I withdraw in USDT?

Check cashier for available withdrawal methods. IBAN is common. Ask @BatrynOrooSupport.

How do Mongolians buy USDT?

Many use crypto exchanges (Binance, OKX, local P2P). Bank policy on crypto can change — verify yourself.

Is USDT required to play?

No. IBAN from 10,000₮ works without ever using crypto.

Before first USDT deposit

  1. Complete registration and club approval
  2. Reach 100,000₮ total IBAN deposits
  3. Confirm USDT appears in cashier
  4. Test with small amount first
  5. Save TxID until balance credits

Binance to PPPoker: Withdraw USDT, select TRC20, paste club address from cashier.

Pair crypto with responsible gaming limits. Faster deposits should not mean chasing losses.

IBAN first, USDT second — why?

The 100,000₮ IBAN threshold verifies your account and club relationship before crypto opens. It reduces fraud and wrong-network transfers from new players.

Most Mongolia players never need USDT. IBAN from 10,000₮ handles deposits and withdrawals for the majority of cash game players.

If you already hold USDT on an exchange, the TRC20 path is convenient after unlock. If you only use Mongolian banking, skip crypto entirely.
