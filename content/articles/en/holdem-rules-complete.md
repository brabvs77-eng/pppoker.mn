# Texas Hold'em Rules — Complete Guide

**Texas Hold'em** is the most popular online poker game in the world. You receive 2 private hole cards; 5 community cards are dealt face-up on the table. The best 5-card hand wins the pot. At [Baatryn Öröö](https://pppoker.mn/?lang=en) 9-max Hold'em tables, every hand follows these exact rules.

If you are new to [online poker Mongolia](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/online-poker-mongolia-2026/), Hold'em is the right place to start. Two hole cards, clear betting rounds, and straightforward hand rankings make it easier to learn than Omaha.

## Goal of the game

You win the pot by either:
- Making the strongest [hand ranking](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/poker-hand-rankings/), or
- Making every other player fold before showdown

In a [cash game](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/cash-game-mongolia/), chips equal real money. You can leave the table anytime and rejoin later. There is no tournament bust-out.

## Table structure

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Dealer (D) | Button moves clockwise each hand |
| SB / BB | Small blind and big blind — mandatory bets |
| UTG → BTN | Early, middle, and late position |
| Pot | All chips bet in the current hand |

The dealer button marks who acts last postflop. That positional edge is huge. Read [table position in poker](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/table-position-poker/) after you know the rules.

## Betting rounds

### 1. Preflop

1. Each player receives 2 hole cards face down
2. Small blind and big blind post forced bets
3. Action starts left of the big blind: fold, call, or raise
4. The button acts last preflop

### 2. Flop

3 community cards are dealt. Another full betting round: check, bet, call, raise, or fold.

### 3. Turn

The 4th community card. Another betting round.

### 4. River

The 5th and final community card. Last betting round.

### 5. Showdown

If 2 or more players remain, hands are revealed. The strongest 5-card combination wins. If hands tie, the pot is split. Kickers break ties when main ranks match.

## Actions

- **Fold** — give up your hand and forfeit chips already in the pot
- **Check** — pass action when no bet is facing you
- **Call** — match the current bet
- **Raise** — increase the bet size
- **All-in** — bet all remaining chips

You can only check when nobody has bet in the current round. If someone bets, you must call, raise, or fold.

## Blinds

At Baatryn Öröö, blinds range from **250/500** to **10K/20K**. Blinds are forced bets that start every hand and create action. The small blind is half the big blind.

Example at 250/500: SB posts 250₮, BB posts 500₮. Minimum open raise is typically 2× the big blind (1000₮).

## How hands are made

You combine your 2 hole cards with the 5 community cards to make the **best 5-card hand**. You can use both, one, or neither hole card.

Example: you hold 7♠ 8♠, board 9♠ 10♠ J♠ Q♥ 2♦. Your best hand is a Straight Flush (9 through J in spades) using 7♠ 8♠ 9♠ 10♠ J♠.
