## FAQ

### Do I need to speak Mongolian?

No. Support is available in English via Telegram. Some table chat is in Mongolian, but «gg» and «nh» are universal.

### Can I use a foreign bank card?

IBAN requires a Mongolian bank account. USDT becomes available after 100K₮+ deposited. Contact support for your specific situation.

### Is it safe?

Club model with withdrawals via the official app. Use [pppoker.mn](https://pppoker.mn/?lang=en) links only.

### Best time to play?
