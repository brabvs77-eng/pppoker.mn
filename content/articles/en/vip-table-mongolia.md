# VIP Tables — Who Should Play and When

**VIP tables** mean higher blinds, stronger opponents, and bigger swings. At [Baatryn Öröö](https://pppoker.mn/?lang=en), blinds go up to **10K/20K**, with Omaha 6-max VIP tables running alongside high-stakes Hold'em. Ulaanbaatar evenings and weekends see the most action.

VIP is not status. It is a stake level. Move up only when your bankroll and skills justify it.

## Who belongs at VIP?

1. [Bankroll](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/bankroll-management/) ready — 10K/20K needs **300K+₮** minimum (30 buy-ins at 100BB)
2. Solid [preflop](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/preflop-strategy/) and [postflop](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/postflop-basics/) fundamentals
3. Profitable over a large sample at mid stakes (5K/10K)
4. Emotional control — [responsible gaming](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/responsible-gaming/) limits in place
5. Understanding of [bet sizing](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/bet-sizing-poker/) at deep stacks

Beginners start at 250/500. Do not «try» VIP to learn. You will lose faster than you learn.

## Omaha VIP

Omaha 6-max at VIP = maximum action. Bigger pots, more draws, higher variance.

Requirements: [Omaha rules](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/omaha-rules-complete/) + [Hold'em vs Omaha](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/holdem-vs-omaha/) comparison.

Bankroll: **50–70 buy-ins** recommended for PLO. A bad session at 10K/20K can cost 200K+₮.
