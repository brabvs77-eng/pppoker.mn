# Mobile vs Desktop — Which Works for You?

**Online poker** in Mongolia — mobile or desktop? [Baatryn Öröö](https://pppoker.mn/?lang=en) on PPPoker runs on both. The right choice depends on your goals, available time, and experience level.

## Quick answer

| | Mobile | Desktop |
|--|--------|---------|
| Convenience | High — play anywhere | Home or office |
| Multi-table | 1–2 tables | 4–16 tables |
| Lobby browsing | Limited | Full view |
| Beginners | Suitable | Suitable |
| VIP / high stakes | Possible | Better suited |

Both work — they serve different purposes, neither is strictly better.

## Mobile — advantages

1. **Play anywhere** — bus, café, during a break ([online poker 2026](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/online-poker-mongolia-2026/))
2. **Quick start** — [download PPPoker](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/mongol-poker-download/), log in, find a table
3. **One table** — sufficient for learning [micro stakes](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/micro-stakes-250-500/)
4. **Mongolia evenings** — 1–2 hours from home

### Mobile limitations

- Small screen — multi-tabling is difficult
- Lobby and [table selection](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/table-selection-poker/) are restricted
- Statistics tracking limited within the app
- Internet — 4G/5G must be stable

## Desktop — advantages

1. **Multi-table** — run 4+ tables simultaneously
2. **Full lobby** — browse tables, see VPIP and activity
3. **Large screen** — [read ranges](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/reading-poker-ranges/), review stats comfortably
4. **VIP / high stakes** — [10K/20K](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/high-stakes-vip/) demands more focus and screen space

### Desktop limitations

- Fixed location — home or office only
- PPPoker requires the PC app or an emulator (BlueStacks / LDPlayer)
- Setup requires stable internet and a suitable monitor

## FAQ

### Can I play VIP on mobile?

Yes. But focus and screen real estate favor desktop for high-stakes decisions.

### Is desktop required?

No. Many players grind [cash games](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/cash-game-mongolia/) exclusively on mobile.

### What if my internet drops?

Mobile: switch between WiFi and 4G. Desktop: a wired or stable WiFi connection is recommended.
