## Who belongs at high stakes?

1. **Bankroll** — 30–50 buy-ins dedicated to high stakes ([bankroll management](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/bankroll-management/))
2. **Strategy** — solid [preflop](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/preflop-strategy/), [3-bet/4-bet](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/3bet-4bet-strategy/), [postflop](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/postflop-basics/)
3. **Experience** — 6+ months profitable at mid stakes
4. **Mental game** — tolerance for big swings ([psychology](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/poker-psychology-mindset/))

Beginners should not enter 10K/20K directly. Doing so goes against [responsible gaming](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/responsible-gaming/).

## High stakes strategy

### Preflop

- Tighter ranges — [reading ranges](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/reading-poker-ranges/)
- 3-bets and 4-bets are more common
- Position is critical — [table position](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/table-position-poker/)

### Postflop

- [C-bet](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/cbet-strategy/) — more selective
- [Check-raise](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/check-raise-tactics/) — more dangerous
- [Bet sizing](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/bet-sizing-poker/) — pot control matters more

### Exploitation

- Read opponents individually — adjust to tendencies
- [Poker statistics](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/poker-statistics-analysis/) — use VPIP/PFR to inform decisions

## Omaha high stakes

Omaha 6-max VIP means maximum action and larger pots. Study [Omaha rules](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/omaha-rules-complete/) and [Hold'em vs Omaha](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/holdem-vs-omaha/). Bankroll recommendation: 50–70 buy-ins.

## Variance — be prepared

At high stakes, swinging 5–10 buy-ins in a single session is normal. Without understanding [variance](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/poker-variance/), tilt and bankroll destruction follow.

## Table selection

[Table selection](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/table-selection-poker/) matters more at high stakes — finding tables with weaker players. Peak activity: Mongolia evenings 20:00–02:00.

Same limit (10K/20K). VIP is the club label; high stakes is the general stakes classification.

### How many buy-ins lost before stopping for the day?

3 buy-ins lost — end the session. Discipline over ego.
