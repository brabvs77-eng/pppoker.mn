# Micro Stakes 250/500 — Surviving and Learning

**250/500** is the lowest blind level at [Baatryn Öröö](https://pppoker.mn/?lang=en) — the starting point for learning **online poker** in Mongolia and building a bankroll. You can sit down for 10,000₮. But even micro stakes demand discipline.

## Quick answer

| | 250/500 micro |
|--|---------------|
| 1 BB | 500₮ |
| 20 BB buy-in | 10,000₮ |
| Purpose | Learning, fixing leaks |
| Risk | Low — but tilt is dangerous |

Micro stakes = not big money, it is a **strategy practice** table.

## Why start at 250/500?

1. **Low risk** — losing one hand does not wipe out your bankroll
2. **Many tables** — 24/7 Hold'em 9-max always running
3. **Cheap mistakes** — practice [preflop](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/preflop-strategy/) and [postflop](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/postflop-basics/) without heavy cost
4. **Build a roll** — move up once you become profitable

[Cash game Mongolia](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/cash-game-mongolia/) format — not a tournament, leave whenever you want.

## Bankroll — how much to start with?

| Bankroll | BB at 250/500 | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| 10,000₮ | 20 BB | Bare minimum — one table |
| 20,000₮ | 40 BB | Recommended — 2 buy-ins |

[Bankroll management](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/bankroll-management/): 20–30 buy-ins total is sufficient for micro stakes. Start with 10,000₮ and add more as profits grow.

## 5 rules for surviving micro stakes

1. **Do not play weak hands** — follow [starting hand selection](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/starting-hand-selection/) strictly
2. **Position** — playing in position wins more hands ([table position](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/table-position-poker/))
3. **Tilt** — leave the table if you lose 2–3 buy-ins ([poker psychology](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/poker-psychology-mindset/))
4. **Table selection** — avoid overly aggressive tables ([table selection](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/table-selection-poker/))
5. **Stats** — track VPIP and winnings ([statistics](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/poker-statistics-analysis/))

## Common mistakes

- **Playing too many hands** — even at micro you need to fold often
- **Defending the blinds** — calling with weak holdings
- **Reloading repeatedly** — buying in again and again after losses
- **Moving up too soon** — jumping to 500/1K before you are ready

## When to move up?

Conditions for moving to 500/1K or 1K/2K:

- 3+ months at 250/500
- 30+ buy-in bankroll
- Profitable by statistics (or minimal loss)
- Understanding [variance](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/poker-variance/)

Moving up too quickly = losing your bankroll.

## Hold'em or Omaha?

At micro stakes, **Hold'em 9-max** is better suited for beginners. Omaha 6-max has more action and bigger pots. Read [Hold'em vs Omaha](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/holdem-vs-omaha/) before switching.

## FAQ

### Can you make money at 250/500?

Yes, but the primary goal is to learn. Profitable players at this level move up over time.

### How many hours should I play?

1–2 hours per day, free of tilt. Micro stakes is a marathon, not a sprint.

### When can I play VIP?

[VIP tables](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/vip-table-mongolia/) run at 10K/20K and are for experienced players. Master micro stakes first.
