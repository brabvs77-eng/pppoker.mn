# Preflop Strategy — Position and Ranges

**Preflop strategy** covers every decision before the flop is dealt. Correct ranges plus good position = profitable play over thousands of hands. At [Baatryn Öröö](https://pppoker.mn/?lang=en) Hold'em tables, preflop is where most beginners leak money.

Fix preflop and postflop gets easier. Play trash hands and you face tough decisions on every street.

## Why preflop matters

- 70%+ of hands end before the flop
- Weak preflop hands lose money postflop even when you hit something
- Positional advantage starts with which hands you open

Foundation: [starting hand selection](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/starting-hand-selection/) + [table position in poker](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/table-position-poker/).

## What is a range?

A **range** is the set of hands you play in a given spot. Example: opening 25% from the button = a wide range including suited connectors and broadways. Opening 10% from UTG = premium pairs and strong aces only.

Thinking in ranges beats guessing "what does he have?" one hand at a time.

## Ranges by position

| Position | Open range (approx.) | Notes |
|----------|---------------------|-------|
| UTG | 10–12% | Tightest — 9 players behind |
| MP | 14–18% | Medium |
| CO | 22–28% | Wide |
| BTN | 35–45% | Widest — only blinds left |
| SB | Special | 3-bet or fold vs opens |
| BB | Defense | Call based on pot odds |

## Open raise sizing

Standard: **2.5–3 BB** + 1 BB per limper. Example: 3 limpers → open to 6–7 BB total.

- UTG: premium hands only (AA–JJ, AK, AQ)
- CO/BTN: steal with weaker suited hands
- Adjust sizing vs loose callers — they need a bigger price to fold

## 3-bet strategy

When someone opens and you re-raise, that is a 3-bet.

- **Value 3-bet:** AA, KK, QQ, AK — build pots with the best hands
- **Bluff 3-bet:** A5s, KQs, suited blockers — when you have position

Beginners should cut bluff 3-bets. Focus on value and fold weak hands to 3-bets.

## Call vs fold

**Call when:**
- [Pot odds](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/pot-odds-equity/) justify it (especially BB defense)
- Position is strong (button, cutoff)
- Multiway pot potential with suited connectors

**Fold** is often the best play. Folding saves more money than any fancy bluff.

## Defending the big blind

You already invested 1 BB. Defend vs small opens with hands that flop well: suited aces, broadways, pairs. Do not defend trash just because you have a discount.

## Practical rules

1. From UTG open AA–JJ, AK, AQ only (tight table)
2. From the button play more hands, but not 72o
3. Do not open hands you cannot defend vs a 3-bet
4. Track 50 hands per session — watch VPIP and PFR ([poker statistics](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/poker-statistics-analysis/))
5. Review [postflop basics](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/postflop-basics/) for what to do after you raise

## Common mistakes

- Playing every hand (VPIP 50%+)
- Opening K9o from UTG
- Calling 3-bets without a plan
- Ignoring position
- Limping instead of raising (weak image)

## FAQ

### What VPIP should I aim for?

Beginners: 18–25%. Intermediate: 22–28%. Higher on active tables if you adjust.

### Should I limp?

Usually open raise is better. Limping often signals a weak player and invites multiway pots.

### Preflop in Omaha?

Omaha ranges differ — more suited aces, double-suited hands. Master Hold'em preflop first.

### What about 4-bets?

Use with premiums or as polarized bluffs. Only when you understand 3-bet calling ranges.
