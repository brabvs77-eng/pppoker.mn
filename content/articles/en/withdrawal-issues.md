## Common problems

### «Pending» for a long time

- **First withdrawal** — identity verification can take up to 24 hours
- **Bonus active** — wager requirements not met. Read [100% deposit bonus](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/100-deposit-bonus/)
- **Wrong IBAN** — support can help correct before processing completes
- **High volume periods** — rare delays during peak hours

### Money not received

1. Check withdrawal status inside PPPoker
2. Verify IBAN digits match your bank account exactly
3. Confirm bank account is in your legal name
4. After 4+ hours → [@BatrynOrooSupport](https://t.me/BatrynOrooSupport) with screenshot

### «Withdrawal rejected»

- Account not fully verified
- Bonus wager incomplete
- Daily or weekly limit exceeded
- Suspicious activity flag — contact support

### Smaller amount than expected

Fees, bonus deductions, or rake may apply. Full breakdown shows in cashier history.

## FAQ

### How many withdrawals per day?

Club limits may apply. Ask support for current policy.

### Can I withdraw to someone else's IBAN?

Usually only accounts registered in your own name.

### Can I play while withdrawal is pending?

Yes. After submitting, remaining chips stay playable (per club policy).

### What if I entered wrong IBAN?

Contact support immediately. If not yet processed, they may be able to cancel and fix.

### First withdrawal slower?

Yes. Verification on first payout is normal. Subsequent withdrawals are usually faster.
