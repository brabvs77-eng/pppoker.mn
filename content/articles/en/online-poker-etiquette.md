# Online Poker Etiquette

**Online poker etiquette** — chat, timing, and respect for other players — builds a better community. At [Baatryn Öröö](https://pppoker.mn/?lang=en), respectful players stay longer and get more out of the game.

## Quick answer

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Respectful chat | Abusive or insulting language |
| Act within time | Stall repeatedly |
| Stay composed after wins/losses | Post bad beat stories, overshare |
| Announce when leaving | Abrupt exit (though allowed) |

Etiquette = a better player and a better club for everyone.

## Chat rules

### Acceptable

- «gg» (good game), «nh» (nice hand)
- Questions — rules, withdrawals ([withdrawal guide](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/withdrawal-issues/))
- Respectful greetings

### Not acceptable

- Abusive or offensive language
- Telling other players what to do («just fold», «bad call»)
- Asking for personal information
- Spam or advertising

Violations can result in restrictions under club rules. [Responsible gaming](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/responsible-gaming/).

## Respecting the clock

- **Act within 15–30 seconds** on routine decisions. Stalling makes everyone wait unnecessarily.
- **Time bank** — use it for genuinely difficult decisions. Do not use it on every hand.
- **Disconnect** — if your internet drops, PPPoker holds your seat briefly. Reconnect as soon as possible.

## In-game conduct

### After winning

- Do not over-celebrate — «nh» is enough
- Do not coach the player you beat («you played that badly»)
- Remember [variance](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/poker-variance/) — their bad luck is not your skill

### After losing

- Do not post bad beat stories in chat — other players are not interested
- Do not insult opponents
- [Tilt](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/poker-psychology-mindset/) — leave the table, come back later

### At the table

- [Table selection](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/table-selection-poker/) — finding a softer table is fine; coaching players is not
- Collusion (coordinated play between two players) — strictly prohibited
- Multi-accounting (one person, multiple accounts) — strictly prohibited

## Mongolian online poker culture

Baatryn Öröö brings together Mongolian and expat players. Respect looks like:

- Chat that both Mongolian and English speakers can follow
- Awareness of time zones — Mongolia evening 20:00–02:00 is peak activity
- [Online poker 2026](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/online-poker-mongolia-2026/) — the club community continues to grow
