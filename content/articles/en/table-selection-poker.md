# Table Selection — Who and When to Play

**Table selection** is the cheapest strategic edge in poker — playing average on a good table beats playing well on a tough table. [Baatryn Öröö](https://pppoker.mn/?lang=en) runs **online poker** 24/7 with multiple open tables, so the choice is always yours.

## Quick answer

| Criterion | Good table | Bad table |
|-----------|------------|-----------|
| VPIP | High (plays many hands) | Low (tight ranges) |
| Player type | 2–4 weak players | 5+ regulars |
| Pot size | Large, multiway | Small, heads-up |
| Time | Evening, weekends | Early morning |

Picking a table = finding profitable opponents.

## Why it matters

1. **ROI increases** — weaker players = more profit
2. **Variance drops** — tough tables create more swings
3. **Easier to learn** — works even at [micro stakes](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/micro-stakes-250-500/)
4. **Saves time** — leave a bad table and find a better one immediately

[Cash game Mongolia](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/cash-game-mongolia/) — table selection matters more in cash games than tournaments.

## 5 steps to selecting a table

### 1. Browse the lobby

PPPoker app — review all open tables. Check blinds, player count, and average pot size.

### 2. Check VPIP

[Statistics](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/poker-statistics-analysis/) — a table-average VPIP of 25%+ is good (players see more flops). Below 15% means tight, difficult opponents.

### 3. Player count

- **6-max Omaha** — 4–5 players is ideal
- **9-max Hold'em** — 6–8 players, not overly full

### 4. Time of day

Mongolia evenings 20:00–02:00 — more activity, more recreational players. [VIP tables](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/vip-table-mongolia/) — weekends especially.

### 5. Observe for 30 minutes

Sit down and watch 20–30 hands. If the table is too tight, leave and find another.

## Signs of a good table

- Frequent limps and calls
- Big pots, [multiway](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/multiway-pot/) action
- Players chatting — often recreational players
- Blind steals succeeding

## Reasons to leave a bad table

- 5+ regulars (experienced, tight players)
- All pots heads-up, little action
- Your exploits are not working
- Tilt — you have lost 2 buy-ins and are playing poorly, not because the table is bad ([poker psychology](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/poker-psychology-mindset/))

## By stake level

## Bankroll connection

A good table reduces variance. [Bankroll management](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/bankroll-management/) — avoid putting too many buy-ins into a tough table.

## FAQ

### What if tables are empty?

Wait or try a different limit. Tables open around the clock at Baatryn Öröö.

### How long to stay at one table?

2–4 hours, then reassess or take a break.

### Is table selection rude?

No. It is the most commonly used advantage among professional players.
