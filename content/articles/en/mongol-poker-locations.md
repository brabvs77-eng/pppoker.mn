## Regional aimags

All 21 aimags can join online tables. Each zone has its own angle:

**West (Khovd, Uvs, Bayan-Ölgii, Govi-Altai, Zavkhan)** — remote from capital, 4G is the main link. Evening ULAT 20:00–02:00 is busiest. [West Mongolia poker](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/baruun-mongol-poker/) — regional guide.

**East (Dornod, Sukhbaatar, Khentii)** — Choibalsan hub. Russian-speaking players common. [East Mongolia poker](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/dorvon-mongol-poker/) — regional guide.

**Central aimag (Töv)** — many commute to UB, play from home.

**Gobi (Dundgovi, Dornogovi, Ömnögovi)** — sparse population, 4G still works. [Mobile vs desktop](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/mobile-vs-desktop-poker/) — phone is typical.

**Khövsgöl** — summer tourists add volume. Tables run year-round.

**Arkhangai, Bulgan, Övörkhangai** — herders often free in the evening.

Rural sums need stable internet. If the app drops, try WiFi or stronger 4G — [app errors](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/pppoker-app-errors/).

One club covers every aimag — no need to hunt for a local card room.
