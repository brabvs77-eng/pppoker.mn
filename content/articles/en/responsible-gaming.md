# Responsible Gaming — Healthy Poker

**Responsible gaming** means treating poker as skill development and entertainment, not income or escape. For Mongolian players in Ulaanbaatar and the regions, [online poker](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/online-poker-mongolia-2026/) runs 24/7. Without limits, sessions can stretch too long.

Poker rewards discipline. The same discipline protects your life outside the table.

## Golden rule

Play money = **money you can afford to lose**. Never mix rent, bills, tuition, or family budget with your poker bankroll.

[Bankroll management](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/bankroll-management/): Hold'em 30–40 buy-ins, Omaha 50–70. If you cannot afford that buffer at 250/500, play lower or wait.

## Session limits

| Limit | Suggestion |
|-------|------------|
| Time | 2–4 hours max, then break |
| Loss | 3 buy-in stop-loss per session |
| Day | One major session, not all-night marathons |
| Month | Set a fixed entertainment budget |

Write limits down before you open PPPoker. Decide stop-loss when calm, not after a bad beat.

## Recognizing tilt

- Playing far more hands than usual (VPIP spike)
- Oversized bets to «get even»
- Chasing losses at higher stakes
- Playing angry, drunk, or exhausted
- Ignoring [starting hand selection](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/starting-hand-selection/) rules

[Poker psychology and mindset](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/poker-psychology-mindset/) — tilt destroys bankrolls faster than bad luck.

## When to stop

1. Stop-loss limit hit
2. Tired, distracted, or emotionally off
3. Neglecting family, work, or sleep
4. «One more hand» on repeat for an hour
5. Playing above your bankroll comfort zone

Closing the app is a skill. Use it.

## Healthy poker habits

- Study strategy ([preflop](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/preflop-strategy/), [postflop](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/postflop-basics/)) instead of playing more tables
- Review hands after session, not during
- Celebrate process (good folds) not just results
- Keep poker separate from daily stress relief

## Signs you need a break

- Hiding play time from family
- Borrowing money to deposit
- Playing to escape problems
- Skipping sleep or work for poker

Step away and reset [bankroll rules](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/bankroll-management/). [@BatrynOrooSupport](https://t.me/BatrynOrooSupport) can discuss cooldown options.

## Family and social balance

Tell someone your session limits if that helps accountability. Poker should not replace meals, exercise, or sleep.
