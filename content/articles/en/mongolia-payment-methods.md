# Mongolia Payment Methods — IBAN, USDT

**Payment method** is one of the key factors when choosing online poker in Mongolia. [Baatryn Öröö](https://pppoker.mn/?lang=en) offers **IBAN tögrög** (from 10,000₮) and **USDT TRC20** (available after depositing 100,000₮+ via IBAN).

| Method | Min. | Processing time |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| IBAN tögrög | 10,000₮ | 10 min – 4 hours |
| USDT TRC20 | After 100K₮ via IBAN | 10–60 min |

Reliable payments = a trustworthy club.

## IBAN tögrög

### Advantages

- Mongolian bank — familiar and straightforward
- Tögrög — no currency conversion
- Starts from 10,000₮

### Deposit steps

1. Open the [PPPoker app](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/mongol-poker-download/) — go to the cashier
2. Select IBAN
3. Transfer following the on-screen instructions
4. Double-check the IBAN number is correct
5. Wait 10 minutes to 4 hours

[Deposit guide](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/deposit-withdraw-mongolia/) — full step-by-step.

### Withdrawal

1. Cashier → withdraw
2. Enter your IBAN account number
3. Minimum 10,000₮
4. Confirm the transaction

Issues? Contact [@BatrynOrooSupport](https://t.me/BatrynOrooSupport).

## USDT TRC20

### When does it unlock?

USDT becomes available after you have deposited a **total of 100,000₮+** via IBAN. [USDT guide](https://pppoker.mn/en/articles/usdt-deposit-guide/).

### Advantages

- Fast — typically 10–60 minutes
- Useful for crypto users
- Preferred by international players and expats

### Important notes

- Use **TRC20** network — not ERC20
- Copy the address carefully
- Test with a small amount first
