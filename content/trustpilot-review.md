## English (Trustpilot default)

**Title**

Solid PPPoker club — the website actually explains how to start

**Review**

I have been playing at Baatryn Öröö for a while now. I started on pppoker.mn. Dark layout, no casino banner spam. It is clearly a poker club, not a sportsbook. The site lists Hold'em and Omaha, table limits, IBAN deposits from 10,000 MNT, a 100% first-deposit bonus, and a small academy with rules. That was enough to know where my money was going before I funded an account.

Then the PPPoker app. I installed it from the official source on Android, registered, and got the club ID from Telegram (@BatrynOrooSupport). They approved me quickly. The app UI is not Mongolian-first, but the tables and cashier work. Same account on phone and desktop.

First deposit was IBAN, 10,000 tugriks minimum. They applied the 100% first-deposit bonus. It was not instant, but it landed in the window the site describes (about 10 minutes to a couple of hours). Withdrawal was also IBAN, no runaround. USDT (TRC20) unlocks after 100,000 MNT in deposits; I have not used that yet.

I play cash games, not tournaments. I started on Hold'em 9-max at 250/500 (tables like Darhan and Altai, 20K–50K buy-in), then 500/1K. Omaha 6-max is there if you want more action. Tables run 24/7; evenings Ulaanbaatar time are busier. Telegram support answers at night and on weekends.

One downside: you cannot jump from the website straight into the club. You need a club ID from support. That is how PPPoker clubs work, just know it before you sign up.

The site explains payments and rules, the app holds up in a normal session, the tables feel like real players, and payouts arrive. For online poker in Mongolia, this is what I was looking for.
